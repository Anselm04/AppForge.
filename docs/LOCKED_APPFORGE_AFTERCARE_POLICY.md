# LOCKED — AppForge Customer Aftercare & Support Policy

**Approved by product owner:** 9 November 2026  
**Status:** Locked product and customer-experience specification. NOT evidence of implemented billing, deployed support operations, or profit-validated pricing.  
**Currency:** USD.  
**Change control:** Future changes to this approved commercial direction require an explicit product-owner decision. Do not silently alter plan inclusions or introduce paid add-ons.

## Core promise
AppForge does not abandon customers after code generation. Every delivered product must be genuinely functional against agreed acceptance criteria. Basic post-build help is included, and the customer can choose self-directed, guided, automated or managed launch and aftercare routes. An independently deployed product remains under the customer's control.

## Essential aftercare is included
Any paid AppForge builder plan includes:
- Understandable product handover, source-export instructions and project-specific deployment requirements.
- Plain-language, step-by-step launch guidance and comparative hosting/provider options with estimated upfront and running costs.
- AI self-service assistance within an explicitly published included allowance and access to support documentation.
- Reasonable support and remedies for defects in AppForge's agreed deliverables, consistent with contracts and applicable law.
- Information needed to run or transfer the exported product independently.

**Never charge an extra aftercare fee merely to explain how to access, export or launch a promised deliverable, or to address a fault AppForge is obliged to remedy.**

## Customer-choice deployment and handover
Present only technically viable options: **I'll launch it myself**, **Guide me**, **Deploy for me into my hosting account** (where automation is implemented and authorised), **Manage it for me** (separate paid consent), and **Bring my own developer/provider**. Show provider comparisons and any applicable app-store or domain costs.

Before any charge or resource provisioning, show short cards with:
- **Pay today**
- **Estimated ongoing cost**
- **Who will bill you**
- **Who owns/manages this service**
- **What happens if you cancel**
- **What level of assistance is included**

Require a specific, unchecked acceptance for each optional recurring charge. No bundled consent, hidden bills or preselected paid plans. After deploying, verify actual status, document what was completed, and make responsibilities explicit. Customer-owned provider credentials and billing should remain customer-owned. No exporting TrillionAI's private API credentials.

## When aftercare billing begins
Optional aftercare is offered transparently with the initial plan selection but should ordinarily **activate and begin billing when the customer chooses to use it**, typically at deployment or launch, not automatically on the date they first begin building. Monthly renewal is measured from the subscription purchase/activation date as agreed. Cancellation terms, service limitations and refund obligations must be stated before checkout. A separate one-time assisted deployment may be quoted and accepted instead.

## Ongoing customer support
Provide a persistent product workspace and a context-aware **Ask AppForge** assistant using the user's actual project metadata and deployed components. Help explain releases, subscriptions, hosting, DNS, backups, updates, crashes, platform publishing, credentials, data migration and troubleshooting. Offer a safe escalation path when automated help cannot resolve a question. Operational automation requires least-privilege access, explicit permission, costs/quotas, audit logs and rollback/incident-handling procedures.

The assistant must not invent deployment success, paid pricing, hosting invoices or completed maintenance actions. It must not consume billable third-party services or modify live systems without permission.

## Consumer protection, portability and revenue ethics
- All viable solutions are proper working products; disclose cost/quality/scaling differences honestly.
- Paid is not inherently better: recommend free or open-source where it meets the customer's needs.
- Aftercare and managed operations are optional recurring revenue for **real ongoing value**, never a prerequisite to retaining access to legitimately exported deliverables.
- Customer can migrate between providers, subject to disclosed licence and platform restrictions.
- An acknowledgement or handover cannot waive non-excludable consumer rights, promised fixes or contractual obligations.
- Any later TrillionAI proprietary component subscriptions are separately and clearly consented to.

## Required implementation evidence before release
1. Functional product workspace and accessible support assistant with tenant isolation and reliable project context.
2. Verified onboarding, deployment comparisons, documented after-launch responsibilities and estimated costs.
3. Server-enforced entitlements, transparent AI support allowances, billing lifecycle, charge cancellation and fraud controls.
4. Separate deployment and managed-services approvals with auditable consent and provider-owned billing.
5. Human escalation and realistic service scope/support availability; incident and recovery tests.
6. End-to-end tests demonstrating no hidden charges, false launches, forced hosting lock-in or accidental loss of exported customer products.

**Implementation sequence:** This policy is authorised for development. Release, payment collection and live deployment remain contingent on engineering qualification and verified commercial readiness.
