# Landing pricing transparency

AppForge's public landing page must present subscription pricing and build-cost expectations plainly.

## Customer-facing rules

- Show the standard monthly plan prices before signup.
- Show the included build allowance / compute credits using the existing pricing catalogue.
- Explain that a standard build and a complex build can require different amounts of compute and iteration.
- Never imply that a subscription guarantees every possible product can be completed within the included monthly credits.
- Extra credits must be described as optional, published-price top-ups rather than hidden automatic charges.
- When the customer runs out of credits, AppForge pauses work and lets the customer choose whether to top up, upgrade, or stop.
- Keep billing and entitlement logic unchanged when making presentation-only pricing changes.

This document is a product-truth requirement for future landing-page and pricing-page changes.
